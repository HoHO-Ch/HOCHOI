Neruvail / Privacy Policy

A private voyage.

Effective October 7, 2026

Package: com.neruvail.tides

Local game data

Neruvail stores completed islands, best move counts, the current attempt and preferences locally in Android app-private storage. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information through the game.

No tracking or cloud service

There are no ads, analytics, accounts, cloud databases or network permissions. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for game data.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you reset progress, clear app storage or uninstall. Reset all progress removes game progress and the active attempt while keeping your preferences. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes both.

Support email

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you send through your email provider. We use it only to respond to your request, retain it only as needed and accept deletion requests at the address below.

Children’s privacy

Neruvail does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Contact us if you believe such information was sent through support.

Policy changes

Any changes will be reflected in this policy's effective date.
